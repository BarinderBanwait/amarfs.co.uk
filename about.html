The name

Amar Financial Services is named after Lachhman’s mother, Amar Kaur.

She came originally from Hoshiarpur, in Punjab, and moved to Kurukshetra during the 1930s. A formidable woman, she lived to 95, and her mind stayed strong and sharp until her death in 2017.
