Services

Financial advice

Lachhman gives financial advice on protection, savings, investments and retirement.

Life insurance

Life insurance pays a lump sum or a regular income to your family if you die while the policy is in force. It can be used to repay a mortgage, replace lost income or meet an inheritance tax bill.

Regular savings plans

A regular savings plan puts aside a fixed amount each month, in savings or investments, towards a goal such as a house deposit, a child’s future or retirement.

Pensions

Advice on starting a pension, reviewing the pensions you already have, and choosing how to take an income when you retire. Pension contributions receive tax relief, within limits.

Investment bonds

An investment bond is a lump sum invested through a life insurance policy. A bond can be used for long-term growth or to provide regular withdrawals, and can form part of estate planning.

ISAs

An Individual Savings Account (ISA) lets you save or invest without paying tax on the interest, dividends or growth. There is a limit on how much you can pay in each tax year.

Wills and Lasting Power of Attorney*

Lachhman also helps clients make wills and Lasting Power of Attorney.

* Not regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority.

Wills*

A will sets out who receives your money, property and belongings after your death. Without one, your estate is shared out under the intestacy rules, which may not match your wishes.

Lasting Power of Attorney*

A Lasting Power of Attorney (LPA) lets you choose people you trust to make decisions for you if you become unable to make them yourself. There are two types:

  • property and financial affairs
  • health and welfare

An LPA must be registered with the Office of the Public Guardian before it can be used.

The value of investments, and any income from them, can go down as well as up, and you may get back less than you invested. Tax treatment depends on your individual circumstances and may change in future.
