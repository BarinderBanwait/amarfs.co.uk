Lasting Powers of Attorney

A Lasting Power of Attorney (LPA) lets you choose people you trust to make decisions for you if you become unable to make them yourself. There are two types:

  • property and financial affairs
  • health and welfare

An LPA must be registered with the Office of the Public Guardian before it can be used.
