Privacy

This website does not use cookies and does not track visitors. It is hosted by GitHub, which keeps standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security purposes. Text fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when a page loads.

If you use the contact form, we receive your name, email address, phone number (if you give it), the topic you choose and your message. The form is processed by Web3Forms, which passes it to us by email. We use these details only to reply to your enquiry. We do not share them or use them for marketing.

To ask what information we hold about you, or to have it deleted, email lsbanwait@amarfs.co.uk.
